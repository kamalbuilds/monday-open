# MONDAY OPEN (ui/) references

The live UI is kaverimeshram's design (github.com/kaverimeshram/monday), used as delivered. These references were captured on 2026-10-10 for this product (Playwright, load + network idle + fonts.ready, junk pages rejected) and are kept here to document where the design sits in its domain. No visual change was made to fit them.

## Jupiter TSLAx token page (product screen)
- Files: ref-jup-xstocks-1440.png, ref-jup-xstocks-390.png, ref-jup-xstocks-tokens.json
- Live TSLAx trading on Solana: chart centre, swap ticket on the right rail, trade tape below. Slate `rgb(9,13,16)`, Inter 16px/500 symbol, lime CTA.
- How kaverimeshram's design relates: same "asset panel with chart + order ticket + recent orders" grammar a TSLAx trader already knows, but on deep green `#031b16` with gold `#f3bd52` and a cream ticket, and a phase timeline instead of a live candle chart.

## Kraken xStocks landing
- Files: ref-kraken-xstocks-1440.png, ref-kraken-xstocks-390.png, ref-kraken-xstocks-tokens.json
- h1 Kraken-Brand 48px/700, tracking -1px, near-white on aubergine, centred hero, purple pill CTA, "Trade 24/5".
- Relation: kaverimeshram's hero is left-aligned Playfair Display with a three-line claim; it answers Kraken's "24/5" by naming the weekend gap.

## xStocks.fi
- Files: ref-xstocks-1440.png, ref-xstocks-390.png, ref-xstocks-tokens.json
- Geist Variable 64px/700, teal-green brand on charcoal `rgb(24,26,29)`, stat strip.
- Relation: the green family is xStocks' own territory; the design's mint `#00d6a0` and deep green surfaces read as part of that ecosystem.

## Flighty (outside domain)
- Files: ref-flighty-1440.png, ref-flighty-390.png, ref-flighty-tokens.json
- A status that resolves at a known time, shown as phases (Preflight, At the airport, After landing), system 65px/700.
- Relation: the design's four-step "How it works" timeline (Seal, Reveal, Clear, Settle) with a current-phase card plays the same role.
